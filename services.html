Why we identify our target clients

Identifying our target companies and clients helps us know who our lending business will serve and understand their financial needs. We aim to provide fair and appropriate financial assistance to employees, small business owners, freelancers, vendors, and other responsible clients who have legitimate financial needs. It helps us offer suitable loan services, reach the right customers, and make sure borrowers can responsibly repay their loans — building trust, maintaining good client relationships, and growing the business while providing responsible and helpful financial services.

Purpose of our Customer Service Policy

The purpose of our Customer Service Policy is to ensure that all customers receive fair, respectful, honest and reliable service. It provides clear guidelines on loan applications, payments, communication, complaints, privacy and customer assistance. These policies help protect both the customers and the business, prevent misunderstandings, and promote responsible lending.
