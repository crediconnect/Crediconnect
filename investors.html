Backing the connection between financial institutions and the people they serve.

CrediConnect Solutions is building a specialized, financial-first BPO in a market where demand for both lending support and outsourced customer experience is accelerating. Here's the opportunity, the plan, and the numbers behind it.

The opportunity

Why this business, why now.

Business Opportunity

Financial institutions increasingly outsource customer experience, but generic BPOs lack financial-sector discipline. CrediConnect fills that gap with trained, screened agents built for lending and account conversations.

Market Demand

Rising fintech adoption and microlending in the Philippines is expanding demand for compliant, empathetic contact center support — especially among underserved individual and small-business borrowers.

Expansion Plan

Launch with a core team on lending and account support, then scale headcount and expand into digital-channel (chat, email) and additional financial verticals as client contracts grow.

Sustainability

Recurring, contract-based revenue from long-term financial partners, paired with disciplined operating costs and a trained-workforce retention strategy, supports steady, sustainable growth.
